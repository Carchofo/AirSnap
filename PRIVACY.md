# Privacy Policy — AirSnap

*Last updated: July 2, 2026*

## Who we are
AirSnap is developed by Carchofo (individual developer). Contact: perkatauro@gmail.com

## Data we collect
**AirSnap does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

We do not collect:
- Names, emails or any identifying information
- Photos or camera content
- Location data
- Device identifiers
- Usage analytics

## Accessibility Service
AirSnap uses Android's Accessibility Service **exclusively** to detect button presses from Bluetooth peripherals (headsets, smartbands, remotes) and use them as a camera shutter trigger.

The Accessibility Service in AirSnap:
- Only listens for hardware key events (button presses)
- Does not read screen content, UI elements or text
- Does not monitor other applications
- Does not transmit any data anywhere

## Camera
Photos taken with AirSnap are saved directly to your device's gallery. We have no access to your photos.

## Advertising
The free version displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalization according to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

## Third-party services

- GitHub (for app update checks — only the app version number is requested, no personal data)
